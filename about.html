Sean P. Bergquist is an ecologist with more than twenty years in environmental project management, across regulatory compliance, ecological restoration, and carbon and climate work. He has led programs with budgets up to $35 million and teams of up to 150 people, and has twenty-five years of permitting experience at municipal, state and federal levels.

He holds a Master of Environmental Management from Griffith University, an MBA from Warwick Business School, and a BA in Environmental Studies from UCLA. He has peer-reviewed publications in coastal and wetland science.

The apps on this site come out of that work.
