result: >-
  A saved photo on the plot's page, with its closure reading and the time
  and place it was taken. One photo gives a single reading; four, one
  facing each direction, give a steadier plot average.
troubleshooting:
  - problem: The photo comes out very dark.
    fix: Move out of direct sun, or wait for cloud. Bright sky behind leaves throws off the reading.
  - problem: The app says the phone is tilted.
    fix: Hold the phone level until the marker turns green, then take the photo.
  - problem: The location will not settle.
    fix: Stand still for a minute. Under dense canopy a location fix takes longer.

1. Stand at the plot centre and open the plot in the app.

    ![Placeholder for a screenshot of the plot screen](/assets/img/samples/placeholder-screen.svg)

2. Hold the phone level at about 1.3&nbsp;m, with the camera pointing
   straight up.

3. Wait for the level marker to turn green, then take the photo.

    ![Placeholder for a screenshot of the level marker](/assets/img/samples/placeholder-screen.svg)

4. Check the reading and press **Save**.
