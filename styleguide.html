Style guide

The review page for the stylesheet: every colour and face in use, the three type pairings, the section markers, and sample pages for the entry templates. It is not in the menu and search engines are told to skip it.

Direction D

Colour

Contrast is measured against the page base and against a panel (the raised surface). Small text needs 4.5 to 1 to meet the usual accessibility standard. Anything short of that is marked in red. The palette is final as of 10 September 2026, so the ochre, muted grey and viridian stay as they are even where marked.

When writing pages

Notation

  • Join a number to its unit with a non-breaking space, so a line never ends on the number: type 0.3&nbsp;m and it shows as 0.3 m. Without it, a narrow phone screen can leave the unit stranded on the next line.
  • Greek letters belong in running text, not headings. Open Sans and IBM Plex Serif carry the whole Greek alphabet, so α, β, σ and χ are safe in body text. Archivo and Spectral carry only Δ, μ and π, so a heading such as “β diversity” would show its β in a substitute face.
  • Use the true minus sign − for negative values rather than a hyphen, so −4.8 lines up with +4.8 in a table.
