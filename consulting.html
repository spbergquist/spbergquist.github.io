Consulting

Designing the measurement, not just taking it. For organisations whose numbers have to hold up in front of a regulator, a funder or a reviewer.

  • Program design

    Working out what to measure, how often, at how many places, and what the result will be able to support once it exists.

    For: anyone standing up monitoring from nothing, or inheriting a program that is collecting data nobody can use.

  • Protocol development

    Turning a method into something a field crew can follow the same way twice, and writing down the reasoning so it survives being questioned.

    For: teams whose results change depending on who took the readings.

  • Custom tool development

    Building the small piece of software a specific measurement needs, when nothing off the shelf fits and a spreadsheet has stopped coping.

    For: programs where data collection is the bottleneck rather than the analysis.

  • Advisory

    Reviewing a design, a dataset or a submission before it goes out, and saying plainly where it will be challenged.

    For: anyone about to defend a number and wanting to know the weak points first.
