## Method

Thirty plots will be placed at random across three stands of differing
density. Each plot is photographed with the sample app by one observer,
then again by a second observer within two days, following the same
four-direction procedure. Neither observer sees the other's readings.

The difference between the two readings at each plot is the measure of
repeatability. The pass threshold above was set before any photos were
taken.
